8. Scientific Honesty & Clinical Evaluation Gap Analysis

Research Ethics Disclosure
Scientific Rigor Disclaimer: Database completeness and structured evidence formatting do not inherently demonstrate clinical diagnostic accuracy. MedAuth-VLM adheres to strict scientific honesty protocols:
  • No Hallucinated Benchmarks: Traditional classification metrics (F1-score, Precision, Recall, ROC-AUC) and generation metrics (BLEU, ROUGE) are classified as NOT EVALUATED pending prospective blinded evaluation against radiologist panel ground truth.
  • Zero Synthetic Data: No missing clinical findings, placeholder records, or simulated confidence scores have been created to fill incomplete batch slices.
  • Score Grounding Protections: For cases where original radiology dictation is empty (has_source_text = False), policy compliance scores are capped at a maximum of 40/100 to prevent ungrounded AI approval.
